10 — Hostile markup

Expect: graceful degradation, not failure. Fields with a usable question resolve normally; fields with nothing at all are still stored with their answer and simply carry no label. What must never happen is a confidently wrong label — a field inheriting the previous question's text, or a CSS rule, or a generic placeholder being treated as a question.
