07 — Sensitive data

Expect: the excluded fields must be absent from the record entirely, and the redacted ones must be stored with their question intact but the value replaced by [redacted]. Losing "you were asked for an SSN here" would be worse than losing the digits. The EEO block is stored normally — it is the user's own answer — but it is GDPR Article 9 special-category data, which is why retention and delete are first-class in Phase 3.
